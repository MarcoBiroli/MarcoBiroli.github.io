summary: "Best-of-N jailbreaking as thermally activated escape: each prompt carries a baseline safety level and each augmentation a random barrier. Four interpretable numbers fix the whole (N, M) attack surface — extrapolating from N ≤ 100 to N = 10⁴, collapsing five models onto one scaling function, and predicting attack success at unseen temperatures. The apparent power law in N is a finite-size artifact."
