summary: "A capacity bound — latent channel rate against the data's bipartite mutual information — shows that any successful VAE decoder is structurally a finite-size mean-field factorization, so its microscopic parameters can be read off the trained network. Validated on Curie–Weiss, Hopfield and Maier–Saupe models, and on salamander retinal recordings, where two latents suffice to recover the stored patterns of the neural population."
