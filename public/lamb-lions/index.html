What the marks mean

T
Equilateral triangle on the sphere with three angles of 120°. It covers one quarter of the sphere.
c
Centre of T: the three lions are at the same place, the lamb ahead.
V
Corners of T: the lamb meets two lions at once, the third one is behind.
sides
Capture walls. On the side marked lion 1 the lamb meets lion 1, and so on. The small squares are the midpoints of the sides, where the axes q come out of the sphere.
dashed
Mirror lines: two lions are at the same place.
O
Apex of the cone, at the centre of the sphere: all four animals coincide. The cone has three flat walls and cuts the sphere along T.
q
Axes of the q coordinates, shown with the “q coordinates” button under the sphere: solid outside the sphere, dashed inside. Axis qk comes out through the middle of the side where the lamb meets lion k.
circles
With the axes come the three circles q1 = 0, q2 = 0 and q3 = 0. T holds the whole octant where the three q are positive, plus a third of each of the three octants next to it.
dotted
Orange dotted lines drop from the marked point onto the three axes. Where they land are its coordinates q1, q2, q3.
cube
The “Cube” button draws the cube −1 ≤ q1, q2, q3 ≤ 1. Each face touches the sphere where an axis comes out. The three capture walls and the three mirror planes are its six diagonal planes. The walls cut the cube along three face diagonals (thick lines), which touch the sides of T at their midpoints. Seen on the cube, T is the shaded cap: three half faces around the vertex (1, 1, 1). The solid orange line carries the marked point out to its shadow on the cube.
Coordinates: q1 = (x0 + x1 − x2 − x3)/2, q2 = (x0 − x1 + x2 − x3)/2, q3 = (x0 − x1 − x2 + x3)/2, with x0 the lamb. The sphere is q1² + q2² + q3² = 1 and T is the part of it where q1 + q2, q1 + q3 and q2 + q3 are all positive. In these coordinates c = (1, 1, 1)/√3, the corners are (1, 1, −1)/√3, (−1, 1, 1)/√3 and (1, −1, 1)/√3, and two lions are at the same place where two of the q are equal.
